Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy covers the uploader apps of Brightloop Media (Riddle Bang Uploader, Emojiraten, Quiz Bang and Brightloop Shorts).

What the apps do

The apps sign in only to Brightloop Media's own YouTube channels. They upload our own videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read public view counts of our own videos.

Data we access and keep

  • We access only our own channels' data through the YouTube Data API.
  • We do not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or any other person.
  • The sign-in tokens are stored privately on our own computer and are used only to upload to our channels.
  • We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or give it to anyone.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The apps use YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
